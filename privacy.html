Memory Muscle Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 28, 2026

The short version: Memory Muscle does not require an account, does not collect your name, email, or contacts, does not track you across other apps or websites, and does not sell your data. Your scores and progress are stored on your device.

Who we are

Memory Muscle ("the app") is developed and published by Bordosan LLC ("we," "us," or "our"). This policy explains what information the app handles and how.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your exercise results, levels, difficulty settings, and progress history locally on your device so you can track your performance over time. This information stays on your device and is not sent to us. Deleting the app removes it.

Information we do not collect

  • We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, or other contact details.
  • We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, or health data.
  • The app does not use your camera or microphone. Audio in the Auditory Memory exercise is played to you; nothing is recorded.
  • We do not use advertising, advertising identifiers, or third-party analytics, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Purchases

If the app offers in-app purchases or subscriptions, payments are processed entirely by Apple through your Apple ID. We never see or receive your payment card or billing details. Apple's handling of purchase information is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Crash and diagnostic data

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone settings, Apple may provide us with anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. These do not identify you, and you can turn this off at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements.

Images and faces in exercises

Photos and faces shown in exercises such as Face Memory and Mind's Eye are bundled with the app or generated for it. They are not taken from your device.

Children's privacy

Memory Muscle is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Because the app does not collect personal information, no such information is stored by us.

Your choices and rights

Because we do not collect personal information, we do not hold any data about you to access, correct, or delete. You can erase all locally stored progress at any time by deleting the app. Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR or California privacy laws), you may have additional rights; you can contact us with any request and we will respond.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the app changes. We will post the updated version on this page and change the effective date above.

Contact us

Questions about this policy can be sent to Bordosan LLC at bordosanllc@proton.me.
